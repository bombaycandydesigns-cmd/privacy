TrackSpence! Privacy Policy

Bombay Candy Designs

Last updated: 22 September 2026

TrackSpence! collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to send anything. The app is built without the Android INTERNET permission. That is not a promise about how we behave — it is a restriction enforced by the operating system. Without that permission, the app cannot open a network connection at all.

What the app reads

TrackSpence! reads SMS messages on your phone in order to recognise spending and income, so that you do not have to type entries by hand.

  • Only messages from bank and payment-service senders are read. Messages from people are ignored before anything is examined.
  • Reading happens entirely on your device. No message, or any part of one, is transmitted anywhere.
  • The app stores the amount, date, merchant and category it works out from a message, along with the message text it came from, in a database on your phone.

Other information the app holds

  • Entries you add or edit yourself, such as cash spending, categories and notes.
  • A profile name and optional photo, if you choose to set one. A photo you pick is copied into the app's own storage on your device.
  • Your settings, such as your monthly limit, the day your month starts, and reminder times.

All of it is stored in a database in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

What we receive

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, and no analytics. The developer has no access to your data and no way to obtain it.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics libraries.

Backups, exports and sharing

You can export your data as a backup file, a CSV spreadsheet or a PDF statement. These are created on your device and go only where you send them.

If you choose to share an exported file — by email, cloud storage or any other app — that file then falls under the privacy policy of whichever service you sent it to. TrackSpence! has no involvement once the file leaves the app.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Read SMS / Receive SMS — to recognise spending from bank messages. This is the core function of the app.
  • Notifications — for reminders you set, such as bill due dates and monthly limits. All are generated on your device.
  • Biometrics — to lock the app, if you turn that on. Android performs the check; the app never sees your fingerprint or face data.
  • Photos — only when you choose a profile picture.

The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, microphone or camera.

Children

TrackSpence! is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects nothing and transmits nothing, no information about any user, of any age, reaches the developer or anyone else.

Deleting your data

Open More → Delete all data to erase everything the app holds. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because nothing is stored anywhere but your phone, there is nothing else to delete and no request to make of us.
